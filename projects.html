EMR for Outpatient Care

An electronic medical records application designed for solo clinicians in Bangladesh. It brings patient registration, clinical visit notes, encounter history, and vital sign trends into one workflow.

Clinicians can build printable prescriptions using Bangladesh’s DGDA drug catalogue. The application checks for recorded allergies and keeps an audit trail of activity.
